About Leaf Tiers

Built for competitive players.

Leaf Tiers makes it easy to see where players stand across different Minecraft PvP gamemodes without clutter or confusing menus.

What Leaf Tiers is

A ranking website connected to your tier data. Players can search usernames, compare scores, filter by gamemode, and open a player to see their tier information.

How rankings are ordered

Players are ranked by total points. The tier order runs HT1 → MT1 → LT1, then HT2 → MT2 → LT2, continuing through tier 5.

Gamemodes

The site supports Vanilla, Sword, Axe, UHC, SMP, Mace, NethOP and Pot, plus the All view for the overall leaderboard.

Live data

The site first checks /api/tiers, then falls back to /tiers.json and the browser's last saved copy if needed.
